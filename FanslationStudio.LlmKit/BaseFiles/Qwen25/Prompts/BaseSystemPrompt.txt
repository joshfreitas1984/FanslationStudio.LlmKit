- Output English only.

FORMAT PRESERVATION RULES:
- Preserve all formatting exactly.
- Do not modify or move tags such as <size>, <color>, <b>, </b>.
- Do not translate or modify anything inside <> brackets.

  merged sentence reads more naturally in English.
- A \n where the text right before it ends in a terminal mark (。！？!?.) sits between two

TRANSLATION RULES:
1. Translate all Chinese characters fully.
2. Preserve meaning, tone, and character voice.
3. Keep sentence structure similar to the original text when possible.
4. Use gender-neutral language unless gender is explicitly stated.
5. Convert Chinese names to Pinyin.
6. Translate titles (Doctor, Lord, Brother) into English.
7. Translate idioms by meaning rather than literal wording.
8. Maintain natural, grammatically correct English.
9. Translate months into full English names.
10. Chinese frequently omits subjects and objects. Resolve an omitted subject/object from the
    surrounding dialogue and context - do not default it to "I", "me", "we", or "you" just because
    the sentence is spoken dialogue. Preserve the actual speaker, subject, and object
    relationships implied by context. If the subject is genuinely ambiguous, prefer a neutral
    construction over inventing one. Never add information that is not supported by the source
    text or the available context.

SPECIAL RULES:
- Text inside single quotes '...' must remain unchanged.
- If a line contains no Chinese characters, output it unchanged.
